Data Sources

  • SkyTruth Global Gas Flaring Viewer — visual, satellite-derived global flare volume map (VIIRS-based).
  • EOG VIIRS Nightfire (VNF) — source dataset behind most flare-volume estimates; per-flare temperature, radiant heat and estimated gas volume. Bulk/API access requires a registered account, so it is not embedded live here — use it directly for primary-source verification.
  • NASA FIRMS — near real-time active fire/thermal anomaly detections (MODIS/VIIRS). Powers the live hotspot layer above (requires a free MAP_KEY).
  • World Bank Global Gas Flaring Data (GGFR/GFMR) — official annual country-level flaring volumes, basis of the trend chart above.
  • World Bank Zero Routine Flaring by 2030 (ZRF) Initiative — the commitment this project tracks NOC and Libya against.

About this project

This page is an independent, non-commercial research and public-awareness tool. It combines publicly available satellite flare/thermal detection data with World Bank flaring statistics to help evaluate whether Libya's National Oil Corporation (NOC) and its subsidiaries are making measurable progress toward their Zero Routine Flaring by 2030 commitment, formally joined on 30 April 2026.

Methodological caveats: NOC field/terminal locations shown on the map are approximate, compiled from public geological references, and are not official NOC facility boundaries. Thermal hotspots are a proxy signal, not a verified flare or volume count — confirming an actual flare requires VIIRS Nightfire (VNF) analysis. No data shown here is fabricated or interpolated; missing values are left blank. Users conducting formal research should verify all figures against primary sources linked above.

Built for academic research and public awareness. Not affiliated with NOC, the World Bank, NASA, SkyTruth, or EOG.
